The native reader validates unsupported musical constructs before publishing.
It retains written repeats and fixes TEF3's first-string bar-boundary lookup;
it does not reproduce every TuxGuitar display loss or legacy-provider quirk.
Generated test songs are original synthetic fixtures, not third-party tabs.
